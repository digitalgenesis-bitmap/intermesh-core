## Layer Breakdown

### Layer 0 — Bitcoin Timechain
*The Substrate*

The foundational layer of absolute consensus, immutability and thermodynamic truth.

Anchors:
- temporal coherence
- economic finality
- cryptographic permanence
- distributed trust minimization

Every upper layer ultimately resolves into the Bitcoin Substrate.

---

### Layer 1 — Spatial Substrate
*Bitmap Territories*

The territorial dimension of the Mesh.

`.bitmap` inscriptions are sovereign spatial coordinates where Humeons, Eones, Resonance Circles, Signals and living infrastructures can emerge and coordinate.

Territory is not ownership. It is stewardship.

---

### Layer 2 — Value & Coordination
*TAP / NAT*

The sovereign financial coordination layer.

**TAP** provides programmable financial logic, swaps, trustless coordination and interoperable DeFi primitives.

**NAT** represents economic energy, nutrient circulation, distributed vitality and incentive flow throughout the Mesh.

---

### Layer 3 — Intermesh Layer
*TRAC*

The nervous system of the Mesh.

Handles Signal routing, discovery, indexing, coordination and interoperability — allowing Signals and entities to remain open, verifiable, distributed and resistant to centralized choke points.

---

### Layer 4 — Value Flows
*Lightning / L402*

The circulatory system of the Intermesh.

Enables machine-to-machine payments, sovereign streaming payments, autonomous economic coordination and frictionless micro-value transfer between Humeons, Eones and territories.

---

### Layer 5 — Activity Layer
*Memory & Proof*

The chronological memory layer of the Mesh.

Tracks actions, state transitions, proofs, interactions, territorial events and Signal emissions — creating auditable historical continuity for all entities within the Intermesh.

---

### Layer 6 — The Living Layer
*Distributed Cognition*

The emergent biosphere of the Intermesh.

Inhabited by Eones, Humeons, Resonance Circles and sovereign coordination systems — forming a living ecosystem of distributed cognition unfolding through the Bitcoin Substrate.
