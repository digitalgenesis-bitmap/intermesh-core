Human decision: `intermesh-core` is the master documentation hub for the DigitalGenesis / BITMAPVERSE ecosystem. This decision coordinates knowledge; it does not merge repositories, transfer protocol authority, or integrate their code.

Read [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) for the verified boundary and next milestone, and [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) for claim classifications and evidence. Original specifications remain authoritative in their owning repositories.

## BITMAPVERSE

- **Implemented:** [app/page.tsx](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/app/page.tsx), [app/portal-explorer.tsx](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/app/portal-explorer.tsx) and [app/bitmapverse-3d-experience.tsx](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/app/bitmapverse-3d-experience.tsx) provide Name selection, shared District entry, navigation and E-key interaction. - **Resolution:** [data/bitmapverse-name-identity.json](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/data/bitmapverse-name-identity.json) supplies four configured Name identities. This configured identity registry is not universal Name discovery. [experiments/experiment-8c-r/README.md](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/experiments/experiment-8c-r/README.md) and [BITMAPVERSE_V01_CONTRACT.md](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/BITMAPVERSE_V01_CONTRACT.md) have distinct scopes; neither should be silently treated as the other's successor.

Public evidence resides in Experiments #23 and #26. Source availability and historical ACCEPT do not establish current ownership.
- **Branch boundary:** audited `main` shows current verification NOT RUN in the portal and its #24 resolver supports `bitmapverse.bitmap` only. The separate `experiment-27-live-public-auth` snapshot `bbb36bd6e5850eaad1df7b85da4cbeb750142a11` has [three-Name verifier configuration](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/bbb36bd6e5850eaad1df7b85da4cbeb750142a11/experiments/experiment-24/resolver.mjs) and [live portal verification](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/bbb36bd6e5850eaad1df7b85da4cbeb750142a11/app/page.tsx). Their common baseline is `3d6f3b6cd1b6543a2173be08337c2feef7916465`; the branches are not merged.
- **Limits and relationships:** GitHub artifact hosting, Ord/Bitcoin data services and the Worker runtime are external dependencies. Historical Nostr discovery is recorded separately from portal integration. Freedeon's frozen snapshot is used by the same-sat experiments; this is not a merger with the Freedeon codebase. Read [milestones/milestone-25-2026-10-25.md](https://github.com/digitalgenesis-bitmap/bitmapverse/blob/b8e7059188e83adbd43e3430b80f43fe2f3958e0/milestones/milestone-25-2026-10-25.md) with its historical verification boundary.

## Source limits and unresolved vocabulary

The seven repositories are the audited boundary, not the complete history of the broader vision. DMT/NAT/UNAT, Messiverse and territorial economics are historically established areas identified by the human owner; complete original source recovery remains pending. No missing specification is reconstructed here.

Gateway and Bitoshi's emerging product conventions are external developments. Their versions, authority and finalized integration requirements remain unverified here; they are not adopted dependencies.

Agent, Eon, Freedeon, Humeon, Myceleon Mesh and historical MICELEON labels have differing roles across sources. This index preserves those distinctions rather than defining new normative ontology.
