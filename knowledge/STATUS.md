## Vision, ontology and external developments

DMT/NAT/UNAT, Messiverse and territorial economics are historically established areas of the broader ecosystem vision. Their complete original sources and normative relationships remain to be recovered. The current repository subset must not be mistaken for the entire historical vision.

Gateway and Bitoshi's emerging product conventions are external developments, not finalized integrated dependencies. Their precise versioned requirements and compatibility have not been established here.

Agent/Eon/Freedeon roles, Myceleon Mesh and historical MICELEON terminology are not fully reconciled across repositories. This hub records the differences; it does not impose a new ontology. Residency, renewal, ownership and economic mechanisms remain unimplemented proposals unless independently sourced otherwise. No replacement rewards or guaranteed benefits are promised.
